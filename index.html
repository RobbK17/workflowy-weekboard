Filters

Words must all match (name or note). "exact phrase", -word leaves matches out, #tag and @person match whole words.
p: priority: p:1 p:1-2 p:none p:any. Set it with !p1…!p4 in a task.
due: the calendar day it sits on: today tomorrow overdue (last 60 days) week nextweek lastweek 7d 2026-11-04 2026-11-01..2026-11-15 none (not on a day) any.
est: time estimate: est:none est:any est:<30m est:>=1h. Set it with ~30m or ~1h30m in a task.
is: is:open (default) is:done is:any; is:all includes bullets, not just tasks. under: under:"Project Alpha" or -under:Archive (any line above it).
